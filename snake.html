How to Play

  • Goal: Eat as much food as possible without colliding with the walls or yourself.
  • Controls: Use arrow keys to change the snake's direction.
  • Growth: The snake grows longer each time it eats food.
  • Speed: The snake moves faster as your score increases.
  • Game Over: The game ends when the snake hits a wall or itself.

Tip: Plan your path carefully and avoid getting trapped in tight spaces. Try to leave yourself room to maneuver as the snake gets longer.
