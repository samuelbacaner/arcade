Minesweeper game will be implemented here.

How to Play

  • Goal: Uncover all cells that don't contain mines.
  • Left Click: Reveal a cell.
  • Right Click: Flag a cell as a potential mine.
  • Numbers indicate how many mines are adjacent to that cell.
  • The timer starts on your first click.
  • You win by revealing all non-mine cells.
  • You lose if you click on a cell containing a mine.

Tip: Use the numbers as clues to deduce where mines are located. The first cell you click will never be a mine.
