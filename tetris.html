Controls

  • ← → : Move left/right
  • ↓ : Move down
  • ↑ : Rotate
  • Space : Hard drop

How to Play

  • Goal: Arrange falling pieces to create complete horizontal lines.
  • Movement: Use arrow keys to move and rotate pieces.
  • Scoring:
    • 1 line: 100 points
    • 2 lines: 300 points
    • 3 lines: 500 points
    • 4 lines (Tetris): 800 points
  • Points are multiplied by the current level.
  • Each 10 lines cleared increases the level, making pieces fall faster.
  • The game ends when pieces stack to the top of the board.

Tip: Plan ahead using the next piece preview, and try to keep the stack as low as possible.
